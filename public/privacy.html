Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This notice explains how Zenshuii Ltd uses personal information when you visit stoic-app.com, join the Stoic waitlist, or contact us about the website or waitlist. The Stoic app will have its own privacy notice when available.

Who is responsible

Zenshuii Ltd is the controller for the personal information covered by this notice. We are registered in England and Wales under company number 17601938. Our registered office is Bartle House, 9 Oxford Court, Manchester, M2 3WQ, United Kingdom. You can contact us at support@stoic-app.com.

Information we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. We also record when you signed up and that the sign-up came from the Stoic landing page. Providing your email address is voluntary, but we need it to add you to the waitlist and send updates.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you provide it, and your message and any attachments.

Why we use it

We use your waitlist email address to manage the waitlist and send Stoic launch updates. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you provide when you submit the waitlist form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing support@stoic-app.com. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before you withdraw.

We use enquiry correspondence to respond to you and resolve questions. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing support and managing enquiries.

Service providers and international processing

We store your waitlist email address, sign-up time, and sign-up source in Cloud Firestore, part of Google's Firebase service. Vercel hosts this website and processes technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect it. We rely on our legitimate interest in providing a secure, reliable website for that technical processing. We also use email service providers to handle correspondence. We do not sell your personal information.

These providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted transfer takes place, appropriate safeguards are required, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your information. You can read Firebase's privacy information, Google's data processing terms, and Vercel's data processing terms.

We may also disclose information where necessary to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information only for as long as needed to run the waitlist and provide launch updates. We review the list and delete information that is no longer needed, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any resulting relationship, and where needed for legal, accounting, or dispute purposes.

For this website, Vercel's dashboard keeps request statistics for 12 hours and available runtime logs for up to one hour. Vercel may keep other technical information for as long as it needs to operate and protect its services, resolve disputes, or meet legal and contractual obligations. See Vercel's privacy notice for its retention practices.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy. You can also withdraw your waitlist consent. Email support@stoic-app.com to exercise your rights.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator.

Right to object

You have the right to object to our use of your personal information based on legitimate interests, including for website hosting and security or for responding to enquiries. To object, email support@stoic-app.com.

Other websites

Our company website and the external social platforms linked from this site have their own privacy information. For zenshuii.com, read the Zenshuii privacy notice.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
