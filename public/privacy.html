Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This notice explains how Zenshuii uses personal information collected through the Stoic waitlist at stoic-app.com.

Information we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. We also record when you signed up and that the sign-up came from the Stoic landing page.

Why we use it

We use your email address to manage the waitlist and send Stoic launch updates. Our lawful basis for this is your consent, which you provide when you submit the waitlist form.

Where it is stored

Waitlist information is stored using Firebase, a Google service. We do not sell your personal information. Firebase may process information using Google infrastructure; you can read Google's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as it is needed to run the waitlist and provide launch updates. We review the list regularly and delete information that is no longer needed, or earlier if you withdraw your consent.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw your consent or ask to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing simone.melidoni@outlook.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

Zenshuii is responsible for the information covered by this notice. For questions about it, email simone.melidoni@outlook.com.
